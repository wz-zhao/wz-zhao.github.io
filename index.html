PROFILE

About

I am a direct Ph.D. student at the College of Computer Science, Sichuan University. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Chongqing University, ranking 18/353 (Top 5%).

My research has evolved along one consistent question: how can a multimodal model acquire and use visual evidence more reliably? I approach this from three levels — unified multimodal representation, MLLM perception and localization, and high-resolution evidence-aware reasoning.
