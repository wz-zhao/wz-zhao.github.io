Research Profile

Selected Research

Attend to Anything: Foundation Model for Unified Human Attention ModelingICML 2026 · First Author
  • Unified image, video, and audio-visual human attention modeling within one multimodal foundation model.
  • Modeled general-to-specific cognitive entailment in hyperbolic space and video attention evolution with Fokker–Planck dynamics.
  • Evaluated across 16 benchmarks, with approximately 6% average improvement and 4× video inference speedup.
Samba+: General and Accurate Salient Object Detection via A More Unified Mamba-based FrameworkFirst Author · Preprint 2026
  • Designed spatially coherent Mamba scanning for salient-region sequence modeling.
  • Extended the framework to arbitrary multimodal combinations with graph-based fusion and modality-anchored continual learning.
  • Unified 6 SOD task types across 22 datasets with a single versatile model.
